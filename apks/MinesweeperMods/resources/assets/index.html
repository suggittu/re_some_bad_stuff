4. Move the folder to Dr_L_ARCADE_SND daddy "/ Download /" for the operation sounds.

5. Start BlockLauncher.

6. Click on the wrench, and then "BL Settings" Go to "ModPE Script" and click "Add".

7. Now you need to find the downloaded modes in your device's file system and click on it.

8. Mod Installed!
